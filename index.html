About me

I am an undergraduate researcher at the IRCV Lab, Hanyang University, advised by Prof. Soonmin Hwang.

I study how camera sensor data and image signal processing affect object detection for autonomous driving, especially in low light. My work spans vehicle-mounted data collection, RAW sensor calibration, and detector evaluation.

I am also interested in online HD mapping and closed-loop evaluation of driving systems.
